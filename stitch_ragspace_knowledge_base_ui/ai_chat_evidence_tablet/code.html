Under Article 10 of the EU AI Act, high-risk AI systems must adhere to strict training, validation, and testing data governance practices throughout their lifecycle:

Appropriate Statistical Design: Training datasets must be relevant, sufficiently representative, and to the extent feasible, free of errors and complete according to the intended purpose .

Bias Mitigation Measures: Operators must implement explicit data examination processes to detect, prevent, and correct potential discriminatory biases .

Geographical & Contextual Relevance: Datasets must take into account specific features, properties, or characteristics that are particular to the specific geographical, behavioral, or functional setting.

CHAPTER 2 • REQUIREMENTS FOR HIGH-RISK AI SYSTEMS • ARTICLE 10

1. High-risk AI systems which make use of techniques involving the training of models with data shall be developed on the basis of training, validation and testing data sets that meet the quality criteria referred to in paragraphs 2 to 5.

2. Training, validation and testing data sets shall be subject to appropriate data governance and management practices. Those practices shall concern in particular:

  • (a) the relevant design choices;
  • (b) data collection processes;
  • (c) relevant data preparation processing operations, such as annotation, labelling, cleaning, enrichment and aggregation;
  • (f) examination in view of possible biases that are likely to affect the health and safety of persons, lead to discrimination prohibited by Union law or contribute to impacts referred to in Article 29(1).

3. Training, validation and testing data sets shall be relevant, representative, and to the best extent possible, free of errors and complete.
