Under the finalized EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), providers of high-risk AI systems are bound to stringent data governance obligations under Article 10, paired with substantial administrative financial penalties defined in Article 99.

1. Mandatory Data Governance Requirements (Article 10)

  • Relevant Design Choices: Explicit documentation of data collection origin, curation protocols, and preprocessing methodologies.
  • Bias Mitigation & Representativeness: Datasets must be relevant, sufficiently representative, and free of systemic errors.
  • Special Category Data Exception: Processing sensitive personal data permitted strictly when essential to detect and correct bias.
