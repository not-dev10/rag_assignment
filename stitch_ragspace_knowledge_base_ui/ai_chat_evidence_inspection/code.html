What are the mandatory requirements for high-risk AI systems regarding data governance under the EU AI Act, and what are the penalties for non-compliance?

Under the finalized EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), providers of high-risk AI systems are bound to stringent data governance obligations under Article 10, paired with substantial administrative financial penalties defined in Article 99.

1. Mandatory Data Governance Requirements (Article 10)

Training, validation, and testing datasets must satisfy rigorous quality criteria, specifically requiring:

  • check_circle Relevant Design Choices: Explicit documentation of data collection origin, curation protocols, and preprocessing methodologies .
  • check_circle Bias Mitigation & Representativeness: Datasets must be relevant, sufficiently representative, and free of systemic errors to prevent prohibited discriminatory outcomes .
  • check_circle Special Category Data Exception: Processing of sensitive personal data (e.g., biometrics, health) is exceptionally permitted strictly when essential to detect and eliminate bias .

2. Penalties & Fines for Non-Compliance (Article 99)

Penalties are tiered based on violation severity and business revenue thresholds:

Prohibited Practices Up to €35M or 7% of total global annual turnover, whichever is higher
High-Risk Governance Breach Up to €15M or 3% of global turnover for Article 10 dataset violations
