# Company Policies

## 4. Expense Policy

Employees may claim up to $50 per day for business meals with proper receipts. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence. Approval required from direct manager for amounts over $200.

## 5. Travel Policy

Business travel must be pre-approved by VPs. Economy class only for domestic, business class for international flights over 6 hours. Submit travel requests 14 days in advance.
