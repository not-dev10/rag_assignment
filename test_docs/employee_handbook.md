# Employee Handbook 2024

## 1. Annual Leave

Employees accrue 20 paid leave days annually. Leave requests must be submitted through the internal employee portal at least 5 business days in advance. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried over to the following year. Contact: hr@company.com for approval.

## 2. Sick Leave

Sick leave requires notification to your manager by 9am on the day of absence. Self-certification for up to 7 consecutive days. Medical certification required for absences exceeding 7 days. HR will coordinate with your team for coverage.

## 3. Remote Work

Remote work requests are approved by direct managers. Employees must be reachable during core hours (10am-4pm). All remote work must be logged in the internal system.
