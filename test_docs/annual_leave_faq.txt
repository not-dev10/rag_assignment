# Annual Leave FAQ

## Common Questions

Q: How many leave days do I accrue per year?
A: 20 paid leave days annually.

Q: Can I carry over unused leave?
A: Yes, up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the following year.

Q: How far in advance must I submit a leave request?
A: At least 5 business days in advance.

Q: What documentation is needed for sick leave?
A: Self-certification for up to 7 consecutive days. Medical certification required for longer absences.
